Diagnostics & Auto Care That Come To You

Equipped with pro-grade scan tools, power equipment, and specialized repair gear, Stingo Auto Services handles common failures right where your car is parked.

01

Computer Diagnostics

Check engine lights, ABS/airbag warnings, drivability stumbles, sensor diagnostics, and complete OBD-II live data analysis.

  • Live sensor & ECU telemetry
  • Misfire & fuel trim tracking
  • Pre-purchase inspection scans
02

Brakes & Rotors

Squeaking, grinding, or soft brake pedals repaired in your driveway. Complete pad replacement, rotor renewal, and fluid checks.

  • Ceramic & semi-metallic pads
  • Rotor replacement & cleaning
  • Caliper inspections & slides
03

No-Start & Batteries

Car won't turn over? Rapid clicking or complete silence? We perform on-site electrical testing, jump starts, and new battery installs.

  • Digital load & cranking tests
  • On-location battery replacement
  • Terminal deoxidization & wiring
04

Starting & Alternators

Diagnosing dead charging systems, slipping serpentine belts, failed starter motors, and faulty ignition relays on-location.

  • Starter motor bench testing
  • Alternator charging output
  • Serpentine belt renewal
05

Tune-Up & Fluids

Keep your vehicle running smoothly. Spark plugs, ignition coils, engine air filters, cabin filters, and essential fluid top-offs.

  • Spark plugs & coil packs
  • Coolant & brake fluid testing
  • Air & cabin filtration refresh
06

Roadside & Remote Help

Stuck on the road or in a remote parking lot? Fast assistance for emergency battery jumps, spare tire mounting, and fuel delivery.

  • Emergency spare tire installs
  • Heavy-duty boost jump starts
  • Emergency lockout assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting your car serviced right where it is parked.

That's exactly why we exist! If your vehicle will not crank, makes severe grinding noises, or is unsafe to drive, keep it parked. Give us a call, and our mobile technician brings the testing equipment directly to your vehicle's location so you avoid costly towing fees.
We require a reasonably flat, solid ground surface such as a residential asphalt or concrete driveway, a designated parking stall in an office lot, or an apartment complex parking area with room for our mobile service van. For safety, we avoid steep inclines or gravel inclines.
We source OEM or top-grade premium aftermarket parts directly from established automotive supply partners. If you have already purchased your own quality parts, we can also inspect and install them, provided they match your vehicle's exact specifications.
Light rain can be handled under our mobile service awnings. For heavy downpours, blizzards, or extreme lightning conditions, we will work with you to either service the car in your covered garage or reschedule to the earliest safe window.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Zelle, and cash. You receive an itemized digital receipt sent immediately to your email or phone upon completion.
