Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Stingo Auto Services · Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · Montgomery & Bucks County, PA

1. Introduction & Overview

At Stingo Auto Services (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our customers is a foundational operational principle. We provide premier on-site mobile automotive diagnostic, maintenance, and repair services across Montgomery County and Bucks County, Pennsylvania.

This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, processing, and protection of your personal information when you visit our website, interact with our automated chat features, communicate with our dispatchers via phone calls, SMS text messages, or emails, and receive on-location automotive service. This policy is aligned in all respects with our Terms of Service.

2. Omni-Channel Information We Collect

In accordance with our Terms of Service, Stingo Auto Services reserves the right to collect, record, log, and retain all communications and data transmitted to us across our customer service channels:

  • Emails: Inquiries, service requests, diagnostic histories, and messages sent to our dispatch addresses.
  • Text Messages (SMS/MMS): Inbound messages sent to our business phone numbers, including vehicle photos, dashcam or component video clips, and diagnostic codes.
  • Phone Calls: Spoken information from customer service calls, diagnostic discussions, and voicemail recordings.
  • Online Form Submissions & Web Chat: Form entries and transcripts from our automated web chat interface.

The categories of personal and vehicle data collected include:

  1. Contact Details: Full name, telephone number, email address, and service driveway or workplace address.
  2. Vehicle Identifiers: Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), year, make, model, trim, odometer mileage, engine configuration, and license plate number.
  3. Mechanical & Diagnostic Data: Reported fault symptoms, check engine light OBD-II diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), prior maintenance records, and technician inspection notes.

3. Permitted Data Usage & Strict No-Resale Commitment

We strictly limit the use of collected data to necessary operational and customer service purposes:

  • Answering customer questions, troubleshooting vehicle symptoms, and delivering preliminary guidance;
  • Booking, scheduling, confirming, and dispatching mobile service technicians to your physical location;
  • Procuring correct replacement automotive parts matching manufacturer (OEM) specifications;
  • Maintaining service records to administer our 12-Month / 12,000-Mile Parts & Labor Warranty;
  • Expanding, training, and improving our customer support and mobile dispatch operations.
Zero Data Resale Commitment

STINGO AUTO SERVICES DOES NOT SELL, RENT, LEASE, LICENSE, TRADE, OR MONETIZE YOUR PERSONAL DATA, vehicle information, or communications records to third parties, data brokers, advertising networks, or marketing firms under any circumstances. Your data is used exclusively to provide trusted, professional mobile automotive services.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & AI Processing (Google Disclosure)

To deliver instantaneous, 24/7 preliminary customer assistance and smart mechanical triage on our website, we collaborate with leading cloud and technology providers.

Third-Party AI Processing Disclosure (Google)

When you interact with our website chat Assistant, your chat messages, inputs, and inquiries are transmitted to and processed by third-party service providers, specifically including Google (such as Google Cloud and Google Gemini AI services), to interpret natural language queries and generate conversational automated responses.

Google and our cloud infrastructure providers process this data strictly on our behalf under enterprise confidentiality, security, and data privacy protocols. Google does not sell your conversational data to advertisers or use customer data from our enterprise API requests to train public consumer models.

In addition to Google AI services, we work with reputable third-party infrastructure providers for telecommunications (SMS dispatch alerts), secure point-of-sale payment gateways, and encrypted cloud storage. All such vendors are bound by strict contractual confidentiality agreements.

5. AI Chat Safeguards & Sensitive Data Warning

In direct alignment with our Terms of Service, we maintain essential safeguards regarding automated AI interactions:

  • Automated AI Interaction: You are communicating with an automated software assistant rather than a human technician or licensed dispatcher. AI responses are informational triage only.
  • Accuracy & Pricing Disclaimer: AI-generated statements regarding pricing, repair labor times, hours, and warranties are preliminary and non-binding. Binding estimates require an on-site inspection by a certified technician.
Sensitive Data Warning

NEVER SUBMIT SENSITIVE PERSONAL OR FINANCIAL INFORMATION IN CHAT. Do not enter credit card numbers, debit card details, Social Security Numbers (SSN), driver's license numbers, or confidential medical records into the chat interface. Stingo Auto Services disclaims liability for unsolicited sensitive data transmitted through automated chat widgets.

6. Data Protection, Security & Retention

We implement comprehensive physical, organizational, and electronic safeguards to ensure the security and confidentiality of customer data:

  • Transport Encryption: All web traffic and API interactions are secured with modern HTTPS and SSL/TLS transport encryption.
  • Access Controls: Personal customer details and service logs are restricted strictly to authorized dispatch and mechanical personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • Retention Periods: Customer contact and diagnostic logs are retained only as long as necessary to complete vehicle repairs, honor our 12-Month / 12,000-Mile warranty, and satisfy statutory Pennsylvania automotive recordkeeping obligations.

7. Personal Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to access, review, or request the permanent deletion of your personal records and communication history held by Stingo Auto Services.

How to Request Personal Data Deletion

To request permanent erasure of your saved personal data, submit your request via email directly to:
👉 stingoautoservices@gmail.com
Subject Line: Personal Data Deletion Request

Please include your full name, telephone number, and the vehicle(s) associated with your past repair requests so our administrative staff can verify your identity. Validated deletion requests will be completed within thirty (30) business days.

Mandatory Statutory Exceptions: Certain repair authorizations, safety inspection records, warranty validation sheets, and tax receipts must be retained in accordance with Commonwealth of Pennsylvania motor vehicle laws, business accounting regulations, and statutory limitation periods, and cannot be deleted prior to legal expiration.

8. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

Our website does not use invasive third-party tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or cross-domain analytics pixels.

We utilize standard client-side browser localStorage solely under the key stingo_theme_mode to remember whether you selected System, Light, or Dark mode. This ensures consistent visual styling across page navigations. You may clear your browser local storage at any time through your browser settings without impacting website functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and mobile automotive diagnostic and repair services are intended strictly for licensed vehicle owners and drivers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications to our mechanical services, operational procedures, or applicable legal regulations. When updates are published, the revised “Effective Date” will appear at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website and services following updates indicates your acceptance.
